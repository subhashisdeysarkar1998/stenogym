Frequently Asked Exam Questions

Everything you need to know about the stenography skill tests.

How are full and half mistakes calculated in the SSC Stenographer Skill Test? +
Full mistakes include omitted words, substituted words, and additions. Half mistakes include spelling errors, capitalization mistakes (where required), and missing punctuation. Stenogym's AI grading replicates this exact formula so you always know your true score.
What is the difference in dictation speed for SSC Grade C and Grade D? +
For SSC Grade D, the dictation speed is 80 Words Per Minute (WPM) for 10 minutes (800 words total). For SSC Grade C, the speed is 100 WPM for 10 minutes (1000 words total).
How much time is given for reading the shorthand notes before typing? +
Usually, candidates are given 10 minutes to read their shorthand outlines before they are required to start typing the transcript on the computer. This reading period is crucial for mental preparation.
Are commas and full stops dictated in High Court exams? +
It varies by state, but traditionally High Court exams do dictate "Full Stop" and "Para". Commas are generally NOT dictated; you are expected to place them logically based on grammar. Stenogym trains you to recognize these natural pauses.
